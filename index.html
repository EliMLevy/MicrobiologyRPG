How it works

  • Invaders follow the path to the heart. Stop them before they get there — a breach costs host integrity.
  • Place defenders in the tissue beside the path. Skin barriers go on the path; bone marrow centers release roaming white blood cells.
  • Kills earn ATP; macrophages yield antigen for research between waves.
  • Dendritic cells present antigen so B cells can make antibodies (opsonization).
  • Each pathogen has a real virulence factor. Read its briefing card — the counter is in there.
  • Beat a pathogen once and memory cells make the next encounter easier.
Educational game. Pathogen facts are drawn from standard medical microbiology; mechanics are simplified for play. Not a clinical reference.
